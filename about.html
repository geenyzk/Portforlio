Story

“I enjoy mixing design and development — clean layouts in Figma, brought to life with code.”

I'm a Digital Production, Design and Development graduate with hands-on experience in web development, IT support, databases, and software design. I design in Figma and build with Python, Django, HTML, CSS, Tailwind, JavaScript, and SQL — from first wireframe to final deploy.

I've been freelancing since 2023, building full-stack products from scratch: logins, dashboards, admin panels, booking flows, and order management. In November 2025, I started freelancing with Webflare Studio, and I also completed a placement at Eyres of Worksop, working on IT support, promotional materials, and an internal staff web application.

I'm currently looking for an apprenticeship or junior developer role where I can keep building in a real team environment. Clean code, considered accessibility, and interfaces that feel human — that's what I care about.
